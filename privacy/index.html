Privacy Policy

Effective: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TERVYQ handles information when people use the TERVYQ website, connect a supported social media account, use creator sharing tools, or use publishing and analytics features.

Information we may process

  • Basic account information: platform identifiers, display name and profile image when authorized.
  • Account statistics: public or permissioned statistics such as follower count, following count, likes, views or video count when the connected platform provides them.
  • Content information: identifiers, captions, timestamps, public engagement metrics and publishing status for content associated with an authorized account.
  • Authorization credentials: access tokens, refresh tokens and related authorization metadata required to maintain a platform connection. These are handled server-side and are not intentionally exposed to the browser.
  • Operational data: timestamps, error records, publishing state, security logs and technical metadata necessary to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate authorized accounts, perform requested or configured publishing actions, check publishing status, avoid duplicate publishing, read permitted performance metrics, maintain account connections, secure the service and improve publishing workflows.

Connected platforms

TERVYQ may connect to supported services such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. The exact data available depends on the permissions granted by the account owner and the capabilities approved by each platform. TERVYQ does not use a platform integration to access unrelated account information.

TikTok creator sharing

For the Share to TikTok creator tool, the creator selects a local video, previews it in the browser, chooses posting options returned by TikTok and gives explicit approval before the selected file is sent through TikTok's Direct Post flow. TERVYQ does not add a watermark, logo or promotional overlay to the creator-selected upload. A short-lived signed browser session associates the creator workflow with the TikTok account that authorized access.

Sharing and service providers

TERVYQ does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and platform service providers necessary to operate the service, including hosting, database, storage, security and the social platforms an account owner chooses to connect.

Retention and disconnection

Authorization credentials are retained only while needed to maintain an active authorized connection or meet legitimate security and operational requirements. Account owners can revoke platform access through the relevant platform. Operational records and aggregated performance metrics may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, reliability, audit or analytics.

Security

TERVYQ applies access controls and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information being processed. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may decline platform permissions, revoke previously granted access through the relevant platform, or request help regarding TERVYQ's handling of connected-account data through the Contact page.

Children

TERVYQ is not directed to children below the minimum age required to use the connected platform in their jurisdiction.

Changes

This policy may be updated as TERVYQ's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
