Privacy Policy

Effective: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TERVYQ handles information when people use TERVYQ's website, connect a supported social media account, or use publishing and analytics features.

Information we may process

  • Basic account information: platform identifiers, display name and profile image when authorized.
  • Account statistics: public or permissioned statistics such as follower count, following count, likes or video count when the connected platform provides them.
  • Video information: identifiers, captions, timestamps, public engagement metrics and publishing status for videos associated with an authorized account.
  • Authorization credentials: access tokens, refresh tokens and related authorization metadata required to maintain a platform connection.
  • Operational data: timestamps, error records, publishing state, security logs and technical metadata necessary to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate authorized accounts, publish content requested through TERVYQ, check publishing status, avoid duplicate publishing, read permitted performance metrics, maintain account connections, secure the service and improve publishing workflows.

TikTok data

If a TikTok account is connected, TERVYQ only requests TikTok permissions needed for the enabled features. Depending on approved scopes, these may include basic profile information, account statistics, public video information and direct publishing. TERVYQ does not use TikTok permissions to access unrelated account information.

For the Share to TikTok creator tool, the creator selects a local video, previews it in the browser, chooses TikTok posting options and gives explicit approval before the selected file is transferred to TikTok. TERVYQ does not add a watermark, logo or promotional overlay to the selected file. Short-lived signed browser-session data may be used to associate the creator session with the TikTok account that authorized access.

Sharing and service providers

TERVYQ does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and platform service providers that are necessary to operate the service, including hosting, database, storage, security and the social platforms to which the account owner chooses to connect.

Retention and disconnection

Authorization credentials are retained only while needed to maintain an active authorized connection or meet legitimate security and operational requirements. Account owners can revoke platform access through the connected platform's settings. Operational records and aggregated performance metrics may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, reliability, audit or analytical purposes.

Security

TERVYQ applies access controls and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information being processed. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may decline platform permissions, revoke previously granted access through the relevant platform, or request help regarding TERVYQ's handling of connected-account data through the Contact page.

Children

TERVYQ is not directed to children below the minimum age required to use the connected platform in their jurisdiction.

Changes

This policy may be updated as TERVYQ's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
