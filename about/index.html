About TERVYQ

TERVYQ is an independent digital media project dedicated to concise, verified coverage of artificial intelligence, technology and emerging digital trends.

What we publish

TERVYQ turns relevant technology developments into short-form video stories designed for modern social platforms. The service combines source review, evidence checks, editorial scripting, Arabic narration, subtitles, visual composition and platform publishing.

Editorial approach

Automation assists production and distribution, but the workflow is designed to fail closed when required evidence, media, quality checks or publishing credentials are incomplete. TERVYQ does not intentionally publish unverified material simply to maintain a posting schedule.

Platform connections

When an account owner connects a supported platform, TERVYQ uses the platform's official authorization and API mechanisms. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account owner and approved for the integration.

Independence: TERVYQ is not TikTok, Meta, YouTube, OpenAI or another platform provider, and is not endorsed by them unless explicitly stated by that provider.
