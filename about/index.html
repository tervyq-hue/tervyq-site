About TERVYQ

TERVYQ is an independent Arabic-first digital media project dedicated to concise, verified coverage of artificial intelligence, technology and emerging digital trends.

What we publish

TERVYQ turns relevant technology developments into short-form video stories for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. The workflow combines source review, evidence checks, editorial scripting, Arabic narration, subtitles, visual composition, quality control and platform-aware publishing.

Editorial approach

Automation assists discovery, production, distribution and measurement, but required evidence and quality checks remain explicit gates. TERVYQ is designed to stop a workflow when source confidence, media, technical validation or publishing prerequisites are incomplete.

Platform presence

TERVYQ maintains official channels on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. Where account authorization is required, TERVYQ uses the platform's official authorization and API mechanisms and only the permissions granted for the enabled feature.
