Workflow

How it works

TERVYQ uses a staged media workflow so discovery, verification, production, publishing and feedback remain separate and auditable.

1. Discover and verify

Potential stories are discovered from technology and AI sources, checked for duplication, corroborated where required, and scored for relevance before a story is selected.

2. Produce

The selected story is transformed into a short script, Arabic voice performance, timed subtitles, approved visual media, branded composition and final video output.

3. Quality control

Publishing is blocked unless required production checks pass. This includes media availability, composition validation and platform readiness.

4. Publish with authorization

For platforms such as TikTok, an account owner authorizes TERVYQ through the platform's OAuth flow. The system then uses only the approved scopes needed for publishing and permitted account or video information.

5. Measure and learn

When authorized, public video and account statistics may be collected at intervals and associated with the published story. These metrics help evaluate topics, hooks, duration, presentation and publishing performance.

TikTok permissions

The TikTok integration may request user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list and video.publish where those permissions are approved and necessary. Draft-upload capability may also be available through TikTok's Content Posting API.
