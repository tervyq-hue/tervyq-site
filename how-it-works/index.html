Workflow

How it works

TERVYQ uses a staged media workflow so discovery, verification, production, publishing and feedback remain separate and auditable across supported platforms.

1. Discover and verify

Potential stories are discovered from technology and AI sources, checked for duplication, corroborated where required, and scored for relevance before a story is selected.

2. Produce

The selected story is transformed into a short script, Arabic voice performance, timed subtitles, approved visual media, branded composition and final video output.

3. Quality control

Publishing is blocked unless required production checks pass, including media availability, composition validation, source fidelity and platform readiness.

4. Publish across supported channels

TERVYQ publishes through authorized platform connections and platform-specific publishing flows for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. Each integration follows its own permissions, publishing rules and status semantics rather than treating all platforms as identical.

5. Measure and learn

Where access is authorized, permitted account and content metrics may be collected and associated with published stories. These metrics help evaluate topics, hooks, duration, presentation and release performance.

Creator sharing and platform integrations

The Share to TikTok tool is a real creator-facing Direct Post workflow with explicit user consent. It is separate from TERVYQ's internal automated publishing engine. Other supported platforms use their own official publishing and authorization mechanisms.
